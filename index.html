Nasteru Nest Recorder

Nasteru Nest Recorder is a personal, non-commercial application designed to integrate Google Nest camera devices with privately managed network storage.

About

This application uses Google's Device Access API to access authorized Nest devices and provide a video stream to a privately operated recording system.

Access to Nest devices requires authorization through the device owner's Google Account.
