Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

Nasteru Nest Recorder is a personal, non-commercial application developed to connect authorized Google Nest cameras to privately managed network storage.

Information Accessed

With the user's permission, the application may access device information and live camera streams through Google's Device Access API.

Data Storage

Camera recordings are intended to be stored on privately managed network-attached storage. The application does not provide public access to these recordings.

Authentication

Google OAuth is used to authorize access to supported Nest devices.

Data Sharing

The application is not intended to sell personal data or share camera recordings with third-party advertisers. Google's services are used to access authorized devices.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's Google Account access through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the developer using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
